Privacy

Last updated: 2026.08.27

ncrypt does not use analytics, cookies, or any third-party tracking or advertising. There are no ads and no data brokers.

We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, or any other personal information. There is no account and no sign-up. Your nickname is chosen by you, kept only in your own browser, and sent with each message as part of the encrypted payload.

Every message is encrypted in your browser using a key derived from the channel (AES-256-GCM). Only the ciphertext and a one-way SHA-256 hash of the channel are stored on our servers. Because the channel is the secret, we cannot read the contents of any room. The one exception: if a channel is short or easily guessed, the room is effectively public, and anyone who knows the channel (including us) can read it. Choose a long, complex channel for rooms you want to keep private.

Message history is capped at the last 100 messages per room and is retained for up to 30 days.
