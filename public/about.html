About

ncrypt is an end-to-end encrypted chat room. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server-side reading of your messages.

The channel is the secret. Your browser derives the AES-256 key from the channel, and every message is encrypted before it leaves your device. The server only ever sees a one-way SHA-256 hash of the channel (the room id) and the ciphertext — it cannot read the messages, and it cannot recover the channel.

There is no list of channels — any word you type is a channel. Because the channel is the key, anyone who knows it can join the room and read its history. Choose a long, complex channel for rooms you want to keep private. Each room keeps the last 100 messages, so a reload catches you up.
